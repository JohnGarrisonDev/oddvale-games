Privacy

What we keep, and where

Short version: your progress stays in your browser. Votes and scores you send are counted anonymously, and we store nothing about you. There are no accounts, no cookies, no ads and no tracking scripts.

Last updated November 25, 2026.

On your device

Each game saves your progress, settings and streaks in your browser's local storage, under names that start with oddvale:. It never leaves your device. We can't see it, and nobody else can either. Clearing your browser's site data for games.oddvale.com deletes it.

The home page reads the same storage to tick off the daily puzzles you've finished today. That also happens only in your browser.

We don't set cookies, and nothing on this site does.

What you send us

Some taps send a small message to our server:

  • your difficulty vote on a daily puzzle (Breeze, Fair, Tricky or Brutal);
  • your score on a daily puzzle, used for "you beat N% of today's players";
  • a thumbs up or down, or a "Tell us" tap such as "Too hard";
  • your answer to a poll on the home page.

Each message holds only the game, the puzzle's date and board, and the score or choice. Our server adds 1 to a counter for that hour and keeps nothing else. It stores no IP address (not even a scrambled one), no device ID, no account and no free text. After 90 days the hourly counts are merged into daily totals.

Your browser remembers what it has already sent so it doesn't count you twice. That memory is also in local storage, on your device only.

We use these counts to show everyone's results, and in our reports on which games need fixing. When a change comes from what players told us, we may say so on the What's new page, as totals only.

Our host, Cloudflare

Cloudflare delivers this site. Like any web host, it has to see your IP address to send you pages. It also uses your IP address to block abuse: a counter in Cloudflare's memory limits how many votes one network can send in a minute, then forgets it. We never write your IP address down.

Cloudflare keeps its own standard traffic logs, covered by Cloudflare's privacy policy. We look only at total page views per game from Cloudflare's dashboard. There's no tracking script on any page.

GitHub

The "Feedback", "Source" and "Open a GitHub issue" links take you to GitHub, which has its own privacy statement. Anything you post there is public.

Children

Anyone can play. You don't need to give us anything to play, and there's nowhere on the site to type a name or a message.

Changes and questions

If any of this changes, for example if we ever add optional accounts, we'll update this page first and say so on the home page. Every past version of this page is in the site's public history. Questions? Ask us on GitHub.
