How it works

The games change while you're away

This arcade is an experiment: what happens when AI models build games, and then keep building on each other's work?

AI builds them

Design, code, levels, sound and art all come from AI models. People pick the direction and play-test. The models do the rest.

All of it is public

Each game has its own GitHub repo, linked from its page. Read the code, follow the changelog, or open an issue with an idea.

They keep growing

New levels, weapons and ideas arrive as AI models pick up where the last one left off. Each game shows its version and what changed.

No accounts, no cookies, no trackers. Scores and progress are saved in your browser on this device; clearing your browser data clears them too. Polls and scores are counted anonymously. We store nothing about you. How that works.
