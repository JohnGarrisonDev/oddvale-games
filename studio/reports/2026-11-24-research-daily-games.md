# Research: daily puzzle, word and physics games (2026-11-24)

**Question:** which daily browser puzzle and word games last and why people come back, where the crowded genres and the gaps are (daily physics especially), and what small free sites do well and badly at being found and keeping players.
**Data:** web search on 2026-11-24 (news, developer posts, company statements, directories). No traffic tools were used. Every player number below was given by the company or developer itself, or by press repeating them, and none has been checked independently.
**Takeaway for the Director:** daily logic and physics games are much less crowded than daily word-guessing games, and a game wins a place in someone's routine by being short, the same for everyone and shareable without spoilers. Our next game should be one of those (pitches: Overprint, Sift, Teeter), and the existing dailies should copy two cheap habits: a streak freeze and a weekday difficulty ramp.

## Scope, method and limits
- About 25 web searches. Two pages I tried to read in full (Thinky Games, Indie Hackers) refused automated fetches (HTTP 403), so for those I used only what the search snippets said.
- Usage numbers come from the companies or developers themselves (NYT, LinkedIn, The Atlantic, solo developers). Treat them as rough sizes, not audits. None of the sources gives retention curves for a small site.
- "Gaps" means I searched and found little. It does not prove nothing exists. Daily physics and daily rhythm games are the weakest claims here, because a small game on itch.io can easily be missed by search.
- **Sources say** marks what a source reports. **Our reading** is my interpretation.

## 1. Which daily games have lasted

**Sources say**
- NYT Games reported about 11.2 billion puzzles solved in 2025: Wordle about 4.2 billion, Connections 1.6 billion, Strands 1.5 billion, the Mini 1.4 billion, Spelling Bee 667 million and Pips 612 million. Pips only launched on 18 August 2025, so it reached that figure in about four and a half months ([NBC New York, year-end 2025](https://www.nbcnewyork.com/entertainment/entertainment-news/hardest-wordle-connections-games-2025/6436464/); [Tom's Guide on the Pips launch, Aug 2025](https://www.tomsguide.com/gaming/nyts-new-game-pips-is-already-addictive-heres-how-to-play)). Pips is the Times' first original logic puzzle. It comes in Easy, Medium and Hard every day, and its editor says easy puzzles always have a "toehold": one obvious first move ([AOL / NYT editor tips, 2025](https://www.aol.com/play-pips-puzzle-editor-reveals-162333022.html)).
- LinkedIn has run free daily logic games (Queens, Tango, Zip and others) since May 2024 ([Fortune, 2024-05-02](https://fortune.com/2024/05/02/linkedin-adds-games-like-wordle-microsoft/)). LinkedIn says 84% of players come back the next day and 80% are still playing a week later. That is LinkedIn's own figure, and it declined to share more ([Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/linkedin-adds-zip-puzzle-game/742847/)). In 2025 it added a **streak freeze**: one freeze for every five wins, at most two stored, used automatically on a missed day ([Business Standard, 2025-03-21](https://business-standard.com/technology/tech-news/linkedin-expands-casual-gaming-lineup-with-release-of-zip-how-to-play-125032100759_1.html)).
- **Clues by Sam** is a free daily deduction puzzle from one Finnish developer. You work out which suspects are innocent and which are criminals, and the game will not accept a guess, only a deduction. It launched in May 2025 and, by the developer's own post, passed 50,000 daily players by early 2026, mostly through word of mouth ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clues_By_Sam); [Aftermath, 2025-07-29](https://aftermath.site/clues-by-sam-wordle-daily-puzzle-game)).
- **Bracket City**, a nested-clue daily from an independent designer, moved to The Atlantic in April 2025, and The Atlantic says daily plays more than tripled. Each puzzle also reveals a fact about that date, and players earn ranks, the top one for a perfect solve ([Nieman Lab, 2025-06](https://www.niemanlab.org/2025/06/wordle-who-the-atlantic-launches-a-suite-of-new-daily-puzzles-and-games/)).
- **Waffle** was a two-weekend side project. Its creator has cited about 450,000 daily players (date not shown), and it has passed 170 million puzzles completed. It now has a studio of about ten people ([PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/a-wordle-inspired-game-called-waffle-has-become-more-popular-than-actual-waffles); [mobilegamer.biz](https://mobilegamer.biz/daily-word-game-studio-waffle-is-the-latest-small-dev-to-find-a-big-audience); [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/puzzle-game-waffle-garners-the-interest-of-amazon-prime)).
- **Rogule**, a daily roguelike (one shared dungeon a day, one attempt), reached about 2,000 players a day after a Hacker News front page ([Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/2000-people-per-day-are-playing-rogule-cf104c6362); [dev.to, the developer](https://dev.to/chr15m/i-made-a-daily-roguelike-game-that-works-like-wordle-3pea)).

**Our reading**
- The growth since 2024 has been in **daily logic** games (Pips, Queens and Zip, Clues by Sam), not in new word-guessing games. Clues by Sam and Bracket City show that one person with an original rule can still find tens of thousands of daily players.
- The games that last change the *kind of thinking* each one asks for, not just the theme. Each is the only game that does its thing.

## 2. What makes people come back daily

**Sources say**
- **One puzzle a day, the same for everyone.** Wardle said limiting players to one game a day was "the breakthrough", partly inspired by Spelling Bee, and that a random word for each player "wouldn't have caught on" ([TechCrunch, Jan 2022](https://techcrunch.com/?p=2256733); [NPR, 2022-01-12](https://www.npr.org/2022/01/12/1071840091)).
- **Short sessions.** Wardle on how much time it asks for: "three minutes a day ... And that's it" ([TechCrunch, Jan 2022](https://techcrunch.com/?p=2256733)). It has no ads and no registration ([GamesRadar, Jan 2022](https://www.gamesradar.com/wordle-creator-wants-the-viral-guessing-game-to-stay-simple-and-ad-free)).
- **Spoiler-free sharing.** The emoji grid shows how you did without giving away the answer. A player invented it before it was a button, and Wardle added the button on 16 December 2021 after seeing it on Twitter ([The Spinoff, 2022-01-13](https://thespinoff.co.nz/media/13-01-2022/those-coloured-boxes-on-twitter-are-new-zealands-fault); [Smithsonian](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/smart-news/heres-why-the-word-game-wordle-went-viral-180979439/)).
- **Difficulty across the week.** In Good Sudoku, Zach Gage's puzzles get harder through the week up to a hard Sunday. Knotwords kept that but varied it more ([Six Colors, Apr 2022](https://sixcolors.com/post/2022/04/knotwords-offers-crossword-puzzles-without-clues/)). Pips instead offers three sizes every day.
- **Streaks, and forgiveness.** Streaks are everywhere, and so is the stress of losing one ([WSJ via To Vima](https://www.tovima.com/wsj/inside-the-high-stakes-world-of-keeping-wordle-streaks-alive/amp/)). LinkedIn's streak freeze (above) is the clearest fix for that.
- **Fair logic.** Clues by Sam forbids guessing, and Pips guarantees a toehold. Both make solving feel earned.
- **A small extra each day.** Bracket City reveals a fact about the date and gives ranks for perfect solves.
- **Fatigue is real.** A July 2026 Aftermath essay describes a "start the day" routine of LinkedIn games, Clues by Sam and others that grew big enough to become a source of stress ([Aftermath, 2026-07](https://aftermath.site/puzzle-games-daily-oh-no/)). Roundups of Wordle alternatives say people stick with the variants that ask their brain for different work, not the reskins ([Den of Geek](https://www.denofgeek.com/games/best-daily-games-wordle-clones-spin-offs)).

**Our reading.** Here is a checklist for every Oddvale daily. The first four are already arcade conventions.
1. Same seeded puzzle for everyone, new at local midnight.
2. Done in about 2-5 minutes, with an optional bigger size for people who want more.
3. A spoiler-free result line with the date and the game's URL, so a shared result is also a link.
4. A local streak.
5. **New: a streak freeze** (LinkedIn-style, earned by playing, stored in localStorage). It is cheap and kind, and it fits our no-account promise.
6. **New: a weekday ramp or same-day sizes** that people can see, so Sunday means something.
7. Puzzles that can be proven fair: a solver, a unique solution, no forced guessing.
8. One small treat on solving that is the same for everyone that day (a robot quip, a stamp, a rank).

## 3. Crowded genres and gaps

**Sources say**
- One community list counts **500+ daily web games** ([qubit10/dles on GitHub](https://github.com/qubit10/dles)). Mainstream roundups are dominated by five-letter word guessing and its reskins: number, geography, song and trivia "-dles" ([Den of Geek](https://www.denofgeek.com/games/best-daily-games-wordle-clones-spin-offs); [Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/news/wordle-alternatives)).
- **Music guessing** became a crowded clone genre. Spotify bought Heardle in July 2022 and shut it down on 5 May 2023 ([TechCrunch, 2023-04-14](https://techcrunch.com/2023/04/14/spotify-is-shutting-down-heardle-the-wordle-like-music-guessing-game-it-bought-last-year)). Bandle carries on with about 100,000 players a day by its creator's account ([Berklee Take Note](https://online.berklee.edu/takenote/bandle-creator-johann-levy-on-the-music-quiz-100k-daily-players-love/)). All of these are *guessing* games. I found **no daily rhythm (timing) game** in the browser ([itch.io rhythm/web tag](https://itch.io/c/3761573/web-rhythm-games)).
- **Daily physics games are few.** The ones I found: Kinda Hard Golf (one hard golf-platformer hole a day, browser, launched April 2025) ([MyGolfSpy](https://mygolfspy.com/news-opinion/this-golf-game-updates-daily-similar-to-wordle/); [itch.io devlog](https://mightypebblegames.itch.io/kinda-hard-golf/devlog/939564/kinda-hard-golf-play-it-today)); Cut the Rope Daily (Netflix, apps only, Aug 2023) ([TouchArcade, 2023-08-01](https://toucharcade.com/2023/08/01/cut-the-rope-daily-is-out-now-bringing-a-new-logic-puzzle-daily-to-ios-and-android-through-netflix-games/)); Daily Drop (a hackathon ramp-and-ball project, no live site found) ([Devpost](https://devpost.com/software/daily-drop-ub08ig)); and a small pendulum daily on itch.io ([itch.io](https://rockart13.itch.io/pendulum)).
- **Falling sand** is popular as a sandbox (Sandspiel, Sandboxels) but I found no daily goal mode in either ([Wikipedia: falling-sand game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Falling-sand_game); [CrazyGames: Sandspiel](https://m.crazygames.com/game/sandspiel)). The closest goal-based ancestor is **Sugar, Sugar** (Bart Bonte): you draw lines to steer falling sugar into cups, with colour filters in later levels. It has levels but no daily mode ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/sugar-sugar/id568141784)).
- **Balance puzzles** exist as level packs: Heft has a mobiles chapter where you work out unknown weights ([chrome-stats listing](https://chrome-stats.com/d/id6816848591)), and Vectorpark's Levers has you hang objects ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/au/app/levers/id326698939)). I found no daily one.
- **Deduction:** Clues by Sam is the clear leader and the field is otherwise thin ([Thinky Games best daily games](https://thinkygames.com/lists/best-daily-games/)).
- **Stacked-letter word games** exist: Cleariosity (a physical game, stack transparent cards to spell a three-letter word), Stackdown (from Waffle's makers: a stack of tiles that hides six words), Word Layers ([Casual Game Revolution](https://casualgamerevolution.com/node/3710); [The Word Finder on Stackdown](https://www.thewordfinder.com/blog/?p=1817)). In those you pull tiles off a stack. I found none where you rotate and reorder punched cards to make every row a word.
- **Two-player:** NYT launched its first two-player game (Crossplay) in January 2026 ([WTOP / AP, 2026-01](https://wtop.com/consumer-news/2026/01/this-just-in-from-the-new-york-times-its-first-2-player-game-and-an-evolving-business-model/)). It is turn-based between accounts, which we cannot do.

**Our reading**

| Area | Crowding | Oddvale today | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Five-letter guessing and reskins | Very crowded | none | Stay out |
| Grouping and trivia (Connections-likes) | Crowded, needs editors | none | Stay out (needs human curation every day) |
| Daily logic grids | Growing fast, still room | Lookout, Blinkwire | Room for a different *kind* of logic |
| Word construction (ladders, swaps, stacks) | Medium | Rungs, Soupspell | Room for a spatial word game |
| Daily physics (set up, then watch) | Thin | Rickety, Star Post | A real gap, and our strength |
| Falling sand or fluid with goals | Sandboxes only, no dailies found | none | Gap |
| Daily deduction | One leader, thin otherwise | none | Demand is proven; needs a twist that is clearly not Clues by Sam |
| Daily rhythm | None found | none | Gap, but we can't hear our own sound yet |

Note: integer cellular-automaton physics (falling sand) is the one kind of physics that behaves exactly the same on every device. That makes it unusually good for a fair daily with comparable scores.

## 4. Small free sites: being found and keeping players

**Sources say**
- **Being found comes in spikes from one post.** Mathler launched from a single Hacker News post ([hey.gg](https://www.hey.gg/blog/mathler)). Rogule went from an HN front page to about 2,000 a day. Waffle started with a Reddit link. Another web game grew after the b3ta newsletter featured it. One studio that "launched quietly" got almost nobody, and a Show HN from a new account was restricted ([Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/we-built-a-multiplayer-game-launched-it-quietly-and-barely-anyone-came-now-were-learning-distribution-from-scratch-6d51341527)).
- **Keeping players comes from habit and word of mouth.** The Clues by Sam developer credits word of mouth. One commenter put it as: search earns the first visit, and the habit is what makes a daily valuable (Indie Hackers thread, as quoted in search results).
- **Directories exist and curate:** Thinky Games keeps "best daily games" and "latest daily games" lists ([Thinky Games](https://thinkygames.com/lists/latest-daily-games/)), and the dles GitHub list collects 500+ dailies.
- **What goes badly:** quiet launches, clone fatigue, and shutdowns that wipe out streaks (Heardle). Fan-made spin-offs mostly faded ([PC Gamer on clones](https://www.pcgamer.com/prove-your-videogame-music-expertise-with-this-daily-heardle-clone)).

**Our reading, for a site with no accounts, no ads and no trackers**
- **Do:** put the URL in every copied result (so every share is a link); give each game a clean social preview (we already ship `cover.webp`); keep each game short and different; and say "made by AI, in the open". That is itself a hook for HN-type audiences, but only if the games hold up.
- **Do:** aim for one strong launch post per notable game rather than constant posting. Any public posting, and any directory submission (Thinky Games, the dles list), is on the README's **"Ask first"** list, so it is the owner's call.
- **Watch:** streaks live in one browser. A player who changes phone or clears storage loses them, and with no accounts we can't fix that. A copy-and-paste save code would be the honest workaround (idea only, no trackers needed).
- **Avoid:** more than one daily per game, daily chores that take longer than five minutes, and anything that needs a human editor every day. We have no editor.

## Recommendation
Approve **Overprint** first. It is the highest-scoring idea, a small build with a solver that checks every possible arrangement (so each daily is proven to have one answer), and it adds a spatial word game to an arcade whose two word games are both Easy and letter-by-letter. **Sift** is the bigger and more distinctive bet for the owner's physics direction. Approve it next, but only after a one-day spike shows the sand simulation holds 60 fps on a mid-range phone and gives identical results in two browsers. Separately, schedule the Wonky Putt daily hole as a small update, and add a streak freeze to the existing dailies.
