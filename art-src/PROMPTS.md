# Oddvale Games illustration prompts

Shared style for every image (same as the main Oddvale site):
Flat editorial illustration with bold, simple geometric shapes and a soft grainy risograph print texture. Limited palette, used exactly: warm cream #FBF6EC (background), plum-black ink #17131F, hot orange #FF5B2E, sun yellow #FFC93C, mint green #2FD6A3, electric blue #5B5BFF, bubblegum pink #FF8FC7. Characters are friendly, odd little robots and creatures with mismatched eyes (one big round eye, one small dot eye). Playful, warm, curious mood. Absolutely no text, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks or real brand characters. No border or frame; the art fills the canvas.

1. hero-arcade.png (landscape 1536x1024): A cosy open-air arcade in a strange joyful valley at dusk. Three chunky arcade cabinets with glowing blank screens stand on a hill; odd little robots crowd around them: one mashing buttons, one cheering with arms up, one perched on top of a cabinet, one carrying a giant joystick. String lights overhead, a big yellow moon off-centre, layered hills in orange, mint and blue. Plum-black night sky with a few stars.
2. cover-odd-one-out.png (landscape 1536x1024): One small brave odd robot with one big orange eye and one small dot eye stands in the centre, glowing, sparks flying from its antenna. All around it, a huge crowd of identical grey-blue box robots with two perfectly matching square eyes closes in from every side in neat rows. Bright bolts and star bursts fly outward from the odd robot. Dark plum-black background.
3. cover-wonky-putt.png (landscape 1536x1024): A wonderfully wonky mini-golf course on a hillside seen from a slight angle: winding mint-green fairways, a crooked windmill, a loop ramp, round bumpers, a little pond, a flag in a hole. An odd little robot holds a putter and watches its orange golf ball roll along a dotted curving path toward the hole. Sun yellow background.
4. cover-blinkwire.png (landscape 1536x1024): An odd little robot electrician on a stepladder plugging a thick cable into a wall of tangled angular wires that form a maze of right-angle pipes. Along the wires, round lamps shaped like sleepy eyes are waking up one by one, the lit ones glowing yellow with open pupils, the unlit ones closed. Small spark shapes. Electric blue background.
5. cover-lookout.png (landscape 1536x1024): A tidy field divided into square plots seen from a slight angle. Odd little robots stand on small wooden lookout towers, each peering down a long straight row through a telescope. Chunky rounded dark-green hedges with pink flowers block some of the rows. One robot shades its big eye with a hand. Sun yellow background sky.
6. cover-rickety.png (landscape 1536x1024): An odd little robot drives a wobbly little cart piled high with teetering parcels across a creaking wooden truss bridge over a deep valley. The bridge sags in the middle, one plank is cracking, small bolts pop loose. Other odd robots watch nervously from the cliffs on both sides, one covering its eye. Hot orange sky, mint and blue cliffs.
7. cover-star-post.png (landscape 1536x1024): An odd little robot in a tiny cream rocket capsule loops around three colourful flat planets on a long dotted curving path, dropping letters into floating mailboxes with raised flags on the way to a small space post office with an antenna. Envelopes flutter behind. Dark plum-black space with a few simple stars.
8. cover-rungs.png (landscape 1536x1024): An odd little robot climbs a very tall wonky wooden ladder that rises from treetops up through clouds into a starry sky. Every rung is a row of blank rounded square tiles. A brass bell hangs at the very top. A bird watches from a cloud. Bubblegum pink sky fading to plum-black at the top.
9. cover-soupspell.png (landscape 1536x1024): Seen from above at a slight angle, an odd little robot chef in a chef's hat stirs a giant bowl of soup with a big wooden spoon. Blank round cream noodle discs swirl in the orange broth in a spiral, a few leap out in a splash. Herbs and carrot slices float. Checked tablecloth. Mint green background.
10. cover-overprint.png (landscape 1536x1024): Three odd little printer robots, each with a paper-card slot in its chest, hold up big square paper cards punched with round holes and stack them over a large grid of blank cream tiles on a desk. One robot turns its card a quarter turn, another peeks through a hole with its big eye. Ink splats, a rubber stamp, registration marks in the corners, sheets of paper fluttering. The cards are orange, electric blue and mint, each with its own stripe or dot pattern. Warm cream background. Absolutely no letters or text on the tiles or cards.
